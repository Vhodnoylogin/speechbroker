# CTranslate2 loads cublas and cudnn BY BARE NAME, and nobody registers their
# folders any more - that used to happen as a side effect of importing torch.
# engine/cuda.py looks for exactly these two under `site-packages/nvidia/*/bin`
# and puts them on the search path of the loader; without them work on the GPU
# fails on the very first recognition with "Library cublas64_12.dll is not
# found". A machine that measures on the CPU (`device` in audio.json) does not
# need them.
nvidia-cublas-cu12==12.9.2.10
nvidia-cudnn-cu12==9.25.1.1
